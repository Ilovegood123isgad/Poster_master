# Formats, Sizes, and Carriers

Read in stage 1 (carrier and size) and stage 6 (adaptation). The tables are common reference
values, not a supplier's mandatory standard — confirm against the supplier template or the
platform's current spec before ordering.

## Print sizes

### ISO A series

| Finished size (mm) | Typical use | Note |
|---|---|---|
| A4: 210 × 297 | Desktop sheets, notices, flyers, indoor reading pages | Close reading; can carry complete information |
| A3: 297 × 420 | Shops, classrooms, community boards, indoor promotion | Near-to-mid distance |
| A2: 420 × 594 | Shop windows, event notices, exhibition info | Mid distance |
| A1: 594 × 841 | Exhibitions, campus, malls, event venues | The usual "real poster" size |
| A0: 841 × 1189 | Display boards, exhibition key visual, large indoor | Confirm handling, mounting, and installation space |

### Common non-standard sizes

| Finished size (mm) | Typical use | Note |
|---|---|---|
| 500 × 700 | Shops, community, indoor events | Balanced cost and ease of pasting |
| 600 × 900 | Commercial events, campus, exhibitions | Common portrait ratio; good for mid-to-far reading |
| 700 × 1000 | Malls, exhibitions, event posters | Strong visual impact |
| 800 × 1200 | Display boards, on-site wayfinding | Board and support structure matter |
| 900 × 1200 | Event backdrop, background poster | Confirm orientation on site |
| 1200 × 1800 | Large boards, photo walls, backdrops | Usually KT board, PVC, or truss |

Fix the **finished size** and the orientation first, then the artwork size, the bleed, and the
installation. Do not hand a designer only a pixel count, and do not apply an A-series size to
a roll-up banner, a light box, or outdoor printing.

## Screen and digital canvases

| Context | Common canvas | Design focus |
|---|---|---|
| Phone portrait, social feed | 1080 × 1440 (3:4) | Title and subject first; keep the bottom block off the edge |
| Phone full screen, vertical video cover, vertical ad | 1080 × 1920 (9:16) | Leave room for system UI and platform crop |
| Square, social | 1080 × 1080 (1:1) | One clear focus that survives a small thumbnail |
| Wide web banner, deck cover | 1920 × 1080 (16:9) | Screen viewing; avoid dense small type |
| WeChat article header | around 900 × 383 | Platform spec changes; confirm in the editor before publishing |
| Retail, meeting room, outdoor digital screen | 1920 × 1080 or 1080 × 1920, or the panel's native ratio | Get the native resolution, aspect ratio, and safe area |

One poster rarely serves every channel. Produce the vertical, square, and horizontal versions
separately; a crop is not a version.

## Output basics

- Close-range print usually starts at **300 ppi** at final size. Large-format printing follows
  the viewing distance and the supplier's spec — 300 ppi on a 12 m banner is waste.
- Print is **CMYK** (or the supplier's instruction); screen is **RGB**. Spot colours, metallic
  and fluorescent inks are separate processes.
- Bleed is commonly **3 mm on each side**, per the supplier's template. Backgrounds and
  bleeding images run past the trim line; type and logos stay inside the safe area.
- **Safe area, bleed, and invisible area are different things.** Safe area = inside the trim,
  keep clear. Bleed = outside the trim, for the trim to fall into. Invisible area = covered by
  structure, another sheet, a platform UI, or a mounting frame.
- Fonts are embedded or converted to outlines per the delivery spec.
- Check the final file at 100% at the real physical size. Look from the real distance: the
  title and subject at range, the body text and details up close.

## Physical carriers

| Form | Typical material | Fits | Design and production notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Wall poster | Coated, matte, photo, adhesive paper | Shops, campus, community, office | Confirm wall material, paste method, viewing distance; avoid type near the edges |
| KT board / foam board | KT board with photo mount | Indoor boards, events, temporary display | Light but easily dented and bent; confirm thickness and support at size |
| PVC / foamex | PVC board, photo or UV direct | Semi-outdoor, long-term, wayfinding | More durable; check UV, water, edge finish |
| Roll-up banner | Roll base + PP adhesive or photo | Conferences, talks, shop entrances | The base hides the bottom; reserve the lower area; get the supplier template |
| X / L stand | Print + fibreglass or aluminium frame | Low-cost, short-term, indoor | The frame or grommets occupy the corners; keep important content away from them |
| Framed poster / display board | Frame, easel, mount board | Exhibitions, launches, art events | Standardise the ratio, the mount border, and the viewing height |
| Light box | Light box fabric, film, soft film, magnetic | Malls, metro, shop windows, night | Calibrate colour; account for the brightness and contrast of backlighting and the black point |
| Window / glass vinyl | Back adhesive, one-way vision, static | Shop windows, glass partitions, vehicles | Confirm inside or outside application, light transmission, cut lines, mounting direction |
| Hanging banner / flag | Paper, fabric, PVC, soft film | Malls, exhibitions, event spaces | Read from several directions; confirm orientation, grommets, clearance |
| Table card / acrylic | Folded card, acrylic | Cash desks, tables, meeting tables, reception | Close reading; a QR works here; proof the fold and slot |
| Floor-standing board / truss backdrop | KT/PVC, mesh, truss, photo | Stage backdrops, check-in walls, photo walls | Confirm the on-site clear size, the seams, the obstructions, and the sight lines |
| Outdoor printing / hoarding | Mesh, tarpaulin, knife-cut, PVC | Site hoarding, outdoor events, building wraps | Wind load, fixing points, hemming, waterproofing, and the reading distance all matter |
| Vehicle / bus shelter | Vehicle wrap, one-way film, light box | Transport media, mobile advertising | Work inside the media template; avoid handles, windows, reflections, and safety marks |
| Digital screen / LED | Image, video, motion files | Malls, meeting rooms, outdoor LED | Get the native resolution, refresh rate, duration, codec, and safe area |

## LED is a display system, not a pixel size

Before designing for an LED screen, obtain:

1. Panel native resolution and physical pixel pitch (e.g. P2.5, P3.0, P5.0).
2. Screen aspect ratio and total physical size.
3. Whether the content is a static image or motion.
4. The safe area — the outer pixels are often affected by the bezel, the cabinet edge, or
   uneven brightness.
5. Brightness, contrast, and colour temperature of the screen in its actual environment.

Rules:

- A static poster for LED must be designed at the panel's native pixel grid, then scaled.
- Thin strokes and small type disappear on a coarse pitch. Set a minimum stroke width and a
  minimum type size from the pitch.
- Do not rely on subtle gradients or low-contrast grey; LED panels crush them.
- Check the file at 1:1 pixel scale on the actual panel, not on the design monitor.
- Video needs the supplier's codec, bitrate, and duration constraints.

## Quick selection by context

| Context | Typical size / carrier | Priority |
|---|---|---|
| Shop window | A2, A1, 600 × 900, window vinyl, light box | Far-read title and the offer |
| Campus / community board | A3, A2, 500 × 700 | Completeness and contact details |
| Conference / talk | A1, 600 × 900, roll-up, X stand | Keep clear of the stand structure |
| Mall / exhibition key visual | A0, 700 × 1000, 1200 × 1800, light box, truss | Measure the real viewing distance first |
| Social distribution | 3:4, 9:16, and 1:1 versions | Same hierarchy, different composition |
| Night or far-distance outdoor | Light box, outdoor printing, LED | Brightness, contrast, wind, reading distance |

## Eight things to confirm before ordering

1. Finished size, orientation, quantity.
2. Artwork size, bleed, crop lines, safe area.
3. Material, thickness, surface finish, lamination.
4. Installation structure, fixings, base, hangers, and the clear space on site.
5. Indoor, semi-outdoor, or outdoor — and the water, sun, and wind requirements.
6. Colour mode, effective resolution, font handling, file format.
7. Who transports, folds, sews, mounts, and installs.
8. Who proofs, checks colour, verifies text, tests the QR, and signs off acceptance.

## Effective DPI by distance

A quick sanity check when no supplier spec exists:

| Reading distance | Minimum type height | Minimum stroke |
|---|---|---|
| 0.5–1 m (desktop, table card) | 8–10 mm | 0.3 mm |
| 1–2 m (A3, indoor wall) | 20–25 mm | 0.6 mm |
| 3–5 m (A1/A2 corridor) | 50–70 mm | 1.5 mm |
| 8–15 m (outdoor, large board) | 150+ mm | 4 mm |

The number that matters is the **cap height**, not the font size, and it is measured on the
printed piece, not in the design file.
