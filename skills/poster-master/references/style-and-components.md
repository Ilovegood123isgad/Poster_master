# Style Families and Components

Read in stage 3, alongside `creative-direction.md`. This is a decision aid: it maps
style families to the situations they serve, and lists the components that carry a poster.

## Style families

Pick by the information task and the audience state, not by what looks nice.

| Family | Structure | Image | Colour | Type | Serves | Fails when |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Information minimal / Swiss grid | Strict grid, generous whitespace | Small or none, or one strong photograph | Monochrome + one accent | Neutral sans, strong hierarchy | Notices, wayfinding, institutional trust | The subject needs emotional warmth |
| Corporate / professional | Grid, structured blocks | Formal photography or diagrams | Restrained blue-grey | Sans, tight hierarchy | B2B, government, finance | It becomes cold and forgettable |
| Editorial / magazine | Asymmetric columns, rules, captions | Photography plus annotation | Limited palette, strong contrast | Serif display + sans text | Culture, exhibitions, long reads | Copy is tiny and dense |
| High whitespace / luxury | Minimal, huge type, tiny type | Product or a single gesture | Near-monochrome, one metal or deep tone | Fine display serif, wide letterspacing | Premium goods, restrained brands | The audience expects loudness |
| New Chinese / guochao | Symmetric or grid | Traditional motifs, flat illustration | Deep red / ink / gold | Calligraphic display + clean sans | Festivals, national themes | Mixed with modern tech cues |
| Traditional festival | Centred, layered | Illustration, patterns | Warm reds and golds | Display brush or serif | Lunar new year, weddings | Used for a tech product |
| Warm illustration | Soft shapes, rounded | Flat or soft-shaded illustration | Warm pastels | Rounded sans | Family, community, campus | The topic is serious or urgent |
| Campus / flat comic | Bold shapes, dynamic angle | Flat comic illustration | Saturated, high contrast | Heavy display + hand accents | Student clubs, campus events | The audience is corporate |
| Children / education | Playful, large | Illustration or real photo, warm | Bright, high contrast | Rounded, large | Kids, courses, public education | The topic needs authority |
| IP / co-brand | Character-led | IP character | Brand palette | Brand display + supporting | Youth brands, licensed collabs | Licence is not confirmed |
| Photographic editorial | Full-bleed photo | Photography with grading | Neutral, photo-driven | Small overlay type | Documentary, lifestyle, storytelling | The photo is not licensed or is low resolution |
| Product / commerce | Product hero, price anchor | Product cutout or in-situ | Commercial contrast | Clear sans, big price | Retail, e-commerce, promotion | The product image is unlicensed |
| Data / infographic | Modular diagram | Charts, pictograms | Categorical, accessible | Sans with numbers emphasised | Education, science, reports | The data is unverified |
| Public service / warning | High contrast, directive | Pictogram or icon | Warning colours, high contrast | Bold, unambiguous | Safety, compliance, emergency | Over-decorated; directive unclear |
| Natural / eco | Organic shapes, air | Nature photography or illustration | Greens, earth, low saturation | Humanist sans or serif | Sustainability, outdoor, agriculture | Looks dated or generic |
| Retro / vintage | Distressed frame, halftone | Aged photo or print | Muted spot colours | Period display type | Nostalgia, anniversaries | The subject is new technology |
| Collage / mixed media | Layered cut-outs | Mixed sources | Varied, conflicting | Mixed | Editorial, cultural, underground | Legibility collapses |
| Pixel / mosaic | Modular grid | Pixel or mosaic art | Limited palette, hard edges | Pixel or mono | Games, digital culture, retro tech | The brief is corporate formal |
| Glitch / cyber | Displaced blocks | Digital art | Neon on dark | Mono / techno | Tech, gaming, events | The subject needs trust |
| Brutalist / anti-design | Raw grid, collision | Raw photos | Loud, clashing | Contrasting grotesques | Underground, agency, statement | The client wants corporate calm |
| Hand-drawn / community | Loose line, warmth | Hand-drawn illustration | Warm, imperfect | Hand lettering | Neighbourhood, charity, warmth | The subject is technical |
| Wedding / soft ritual | Symmetric, airy | Soft photography or illustration | Blush, cream, gold | Fine script + serif | Weddings, celebrations | The event is corporate |
| Film noir / dramatic | High-contrast lighting | Dramatic photography | Desaturated, deep shadows | Condensed display | Culture, exhibitions, premium | Low contrast kills screen legibility |
| Japanese light / lifestyle | Asymmetry, air | Soft lifestyle photography | Pale, low saturation | Light sans | Lifestyle, wellness, minimal | Needs urgency or a call to action |

## Components

A poster is assembled from a small set of components. Design them explicitly; the same
component changes with the style.

| Component | Purpose | Changes by style |
|---|---|---|
| Title block | The claim | Weight and case; not always the largest element |
| Kicker / eyebrow | Category or context | Small caps, a rule, a tag |
| Supporting line | The reason to care | One sentence; may sit above the title |
| Key visual | The subject | Photography, illustration, abstract, product |
| Info card | Time, place, price, contact | A card, a band, a column, plain setting |
| CTA | The action | Button, underlined link, QR + verb |
| QR | The action entry | Size, quiet zone, and companion text |
| Logo lockup | Identification | Fixed; never restyled |
| Rule / divider | Structure | Weight and colour |
| Badge / sticker | Emphasis, offer, date | Loud in youthful styles, absent in restrained ones |
| Data block | Numbers and facts | Table, large figures, chart |
| Texture | Material | Paper, halftone, grain, riso |
| Legal note | Constraint | Small, but never removed |
| Arrow / route | Wayfinding | Directional, high contrast |

## Component rules

1. **The CTA is a component, not a decoration.** It gets a verb, a position, and a size.
2. **The QR always comes with its instruction.** A bare QR asks the viewer to guess.
3. **A card is a container, not a style.** A rounded card with a shadow on a printed poster is
   noise; on screen it may be the right idiom.
4. **The legal note survives every layout.** If it does not fit, the layout changes.
5. **Decorative components must be subordinate.** If a badge competes with the title, one of
   them is wrong.

## The style analysis card

Fill this when analysing a reference, so that what was borrowed is explicit:

```markdown
## 风格分析卡

- 来源 / 链接：
- 借鉴的具体点（结构 / 图像 / 色彩 / 字体）：
- 为什么适合本项目：
- 不借用它的部分：
- 该参考的版权状态：<自有 / 已获授权 / 仅作原理参考，不得复用素材>
```

## Reference boundaries

- A reference is a source of a **principle**, not a source of an **asset**.
- Record the source of every reference. "Found on Pinterest" is not a source.
- Never reuse another poster's artwork, layout, characters, or distinctive composition in a
  delivered piece.
- When a style is copied closely enough that it would read as a derivative, change the
  structure — not just the colour.
